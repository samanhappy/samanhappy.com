## Overview

Codex Reset helps Chinese Codex users understand their quota and reset timing, so they can plan their AI-assisted development work with fewer interruptions.

## Who it's for

Developers who use Codex regularly and want to know when their quota will be available again.

## Use it when

- You want to know when your quota is expected to reset.
- You need to plan upcoming work around available usage.
- You would rather receive a notification than keep checking manually.

## Key features

- Quota information and reset estimates.
- An interface designed for Chinese users.
- Timely notifications to reduce repeated checks.

## Why I built it

Waiting for quota to recover interrupts the development flow. A clear place to check reset information makes it easier to plan the next stretch of work.

## Compared with manual checking

Occasional users may be happy to check manually. A dedicated reset and notification service is useful for people who want to avoid doing that repeatedly.

## FAQ

### Is this an official OpenAI product?

No. Codex Reset is independently developed and is not affiliated with OpenAI.

### Are reset estimates official guarantees?

Estimates are intended to help with planning. Actual quota availability and reset times are determined by Codex and should be checked against its official interface.
